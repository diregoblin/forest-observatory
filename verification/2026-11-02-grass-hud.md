# Denser grass and compact HUD — 2026-11-02

## Requested and implemented

- Grass density: 18² → 26² anchored tufts per 8 × 8 m tile, **2.086×** at
  the same submitted habitat/cover masks. This does not create food or biomass.
- Base card height: 0.32 → 0.40 m (**+25%**), with the existing seeded height
  variation. Three fixed crossed planes per tuft, not camera-facing billboards.
- Draw distance: 18 → **36 m**; shader fades from 25.92 to 36 m. CPU tile
  coverage and visibility ranges follow the same distance. Tile bounds intersect
  the camera-local circle; nearby tiles are uploaded first, at most three/frame.
- Persistent HUD: compact title/date/weather, pause, coupled speed selectors,
  and a viewpoint dropdown. The big statistics, journal and key legend are gone
  from the normal view. Individual field-guide cards still open on selection.
- **Options / Esc**: View (style and frame cap), World (rain, barrier, seasons,
  save/load), Observations (maps and full statistics/journal), Controls.
  Options do not change the pause state; opening releases free flight/follow.
  Escape closes the menu. The world does not receive clicks/flight/simulation
  hotkeys from behind the menu. Map actions close the menu to reveal the map.
- Notices remain as short-lived toasts and three entries in the hidden journal.
  The simulation, clock, ownership rules and world-save schema are unchanged.

### Test-fixture correction

`tools/check_pixel_style.gd` previously captured its world baseline before any
scene process callback. The worker-presentation buffer was empty then, and
initialized to the first submitted animal frame during the first render, even
on pause. This alone failed its exact scene-state comparison. Wait for the
first **completed** `RenderingServer.frame_post_draw` before capturing the
baseline; `process_frame` fires before node process callbacks and is insufficient.
The exact comparison remains intact. The test also opens/closes options around
the now-hidden style button, and checks Escape menu/follow cancellation.
