# Грибы: локальная реакция–диффузия, субстрат и плодоношение

Request: Pavel, 2026-10-30, Telegram 7044; add fungi to the existing forest using
the linked paper, including soil nutrition and a feasible spatial resolution.
Clarification: a surface object may be one mushroom or a group per grid region.

## Source and interpretation

Karst N, Dralle D, Thompson S (2016), **Spiral and Rotor Patterns Produced by Fairy
Ring Fungi**, PLoS ONE 11(3):e0149254, CC BY.
<https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0149254>
Read the publisher article and equation MathML from
<https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article/file?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0149254&type=manuscript>.

The paper models a continuum of **mycelial biomass and consumed resources**,
not differentiation of fruiting bodies. Field rings can be observed via fruiting
bodies or vegetation effects; the aerial spirals in the paper are vegetation
boundaries, not direct measurements of underground biomass. The paper itself
emphasizes uncertain parameterization and missing experimental validation.
Therefore we reproduce a qualitative colony-growth mechanism, not claim that
ring-shaped output validates forest fungal physiology or fruiting biology.

A lightweight existing-solution search found Gray–Scott graphics examples, but
no checked drop-in soil/forest model. The project already requires a native CPU
reference and no embedded Python: the implementation adds a small independent
core to its existing GDExtension, with no new engine, framework, paid asset or service.

## Equations and explicit extensions

Publisher dimensional equations (1–2):

```text
r_t = Dr Δr - c1 r b² + g (rmax - r)
b_t = Db Δb + c2 r b² - m b
```

The illustrative reference uses `c2/c1=1`, dimensionless `m=.0475`, `g=.0075`,
`Dr/Db=2`, from Figure 3A (not the inconsistent large values in the Figure 2
caption). The canonical Gray–Scott mapping is **F=g, k=m-g**, not k=m.
The core directly evaluates biomass/resource kinetics, avoiding that ambiguity.

Forest state uses four float64 areal stores, **kg C/m²**:

```text
R: available carbon substrate; B: mycelium; L: organic reserve; P: fruit carbon
U = alpha R B² activity(soil saturation, surface flooding)
J = g (Rmax - R), positive mobilization limited by available L
Q = q max(B - Bmin, 0) fruit_suitability(soil, flooding, temperature)

R_t = Dr ΔR - U + J
B_t = Db ΔB + U - m B - Q
L_t = m B - J + P / fruit_lifetime
P_t = Q - P / fruit_lifetime
```

Positive refill spends L; excess R is returned to L. Default yield=1, no
respiratory loss or external litter input in the forest mode. The code can ledger
the missing-yield fraction as respiratory output, but this default is the paper's
unit-yield simplification, not measured fungal carbon-use efficiency.

The closed-domain check is `sum((R+B+L+P) * dx²) + output - initial - input`.
The reference mode retains external signed replenishment and disables fruiting;
R/B then follow the source model. Its discarded biomass is ledgered into inert L.

**Our additions, not paper results:** finite L and recycling, moisture/flood gate,
fruit allocation/senescence, temperature gate and representative mushroom geometry.
There is no mineral N/P, plant competition for fungal carbon, root exudation,
tree-litter input, mycorrhizal exchange, spores/genetics or explicit hyphal network.
Mycelial temperature response is not resolved. Moisture is sampled from the
existing 4 m hydrology, not magically resolved at 25 cm; fungi do not consume water.

## Scale, initial state and boundaries

Default: three **32 × 32 m** observation plots, each **128²**, **dx=.25 m**.
They advance wherever the camera is; these are isolated finite domains, not
camera-dependent active tiles or a claim of forest-wide fungal coverage.
All four edges reflect, with exported edge biomass and a HUD warning when more
than 1% of a plot's mycelium is in the four-cell boundary strip. No silent wrapping
to the opposite forest edge. Reaching a boundary limits the ecological interpretation;
conservative domain enlargement/coupled tiling remains future work.

The grid resolves the **colony front**, not caps or hyphae. Representative bodies
and clumps are deterministically sampled from local fruit carbon. Exact carbon
lives in P, not the integer mesh count; render rounding, clump-size mixture and
the display cap do not add/remove state. One cell can display a single body or
several smaller neighbours. All mesh dimensions/colours and kinetic scales are data
in `ForestFungusDefinition`, not species-name conditionals.

The SI mapping is explicit but **not calibrated**: `Rmax=.05 kg C/m²`,
`length_scale=.125 m`, `time_scale=30 biological days`,
`Db=length_scale²/time_scale`, `alpha=1/(Rmax²*time_scale)`.
It selects display/scenario length and age, not a fit of observed m/year.

Initial colonies are established, as existing trees also are: point, line and two
point inocula are advanced for **120 × 30 days ≈9.86 years** under homogeneous
paper-reference conditions. The resulting B/R are used as initial forest state;
L starts at the declared `.6 kg C/m²`. Initial fruit (2.5% of eligible B) is
withdrawn from B only where current environment permits it. Forest time then starts
at zero, with all initial carbon inventoried. No ring equation/curve places mushrooms.

Fungi use the fixed ecosystem clock (default 600 scene seconds/year), not water
speed. Pause and T season preview freeze their pools/time. G shows the first
plot's actual B and R fields; 4 moves to that plot. Changing view or opening maps
does not advance or rewrite the model. Versioned snapshots include all pools,
forcing, native clocks/options and plot positions; invalid restores are atomic.
There is still no general user-facing full-world save/load.

Memory arithmetic (eight native double arrays, including scratch/forcing;
excludes scene, snapshots, textures, meshes):

|---|---:|---:|
| 3 plots ×128², 0.25 m | 49,152 | 3 MiB |

Thus a whole-forest 25 cm field is not intrinsically impossible in memory, but
currently unnecessary. Whole-domain costs here are arithmetic, **not measured**
GPU/full-forest benchmarks. A centimetre grid is not needed to render centimetre caps.

## Numerical verification

Five-point conservative reflecting diffusion, positivity ceiling
`dt<=.225 dx²/max(Db,Dr)`, resource-turnover ceiling and first-order operator
splitting. Donor-bounded uptake and exponential first-order transfers, not a final
negative-store clip. Reference half-step/grid comparisons test numerical sensitivity,
not experimental biological validation.

`./tools/check.sh`: PASS, including old hydrology, groundwater/tree ecology,
extension contracts and scene controls, plus new native and adapter fungal checks.
See current machine-readable `artifacts/fungi-native-check.json` and `fungi-check.json`.

Chosen falsifiable controls: the point seed must expand beyond 2 m, a no-biomass-
diffusion counterpart remain under .7 m; dx refinement must change mean biomass
radius by <.5 m and dt refinement by <.12 m at 160 nondimensional time units.
Observed during implementation: approximately **5.88 m** at .25 m grid,
**6.04 m** at .125 m, **5.92 m** with half max dt; no-spread control **.47 m**.
No inoculum gives identically zero biomass; zero resource/no replenishment gives
analytical exponential mortality. Empty organic reserve cannot refill an empty
resource pool. A separately calculated uniform RHS checks the source equations.

Closed carbon residual is below 1e-8 kg in both native and adapter assays.
The adapter continues all pools/forcing exactly after restore, rejects negative
fruit state without partial mutation, blocks new fruit on dry soil, and verifies
that refreshing single bodies/clumps does not spend carbon or advance model time.
Scene controls additionally freeze fungi on pause/season preview.

Core timing for one requested ecosystem step (26,280 biological seconds), local
machine, indicative: 128² ~0.2 ms, 256² ~1.4 ms, 512² ~5.8 ms. Adapter assay for
all three plots ~1–2 ms/step. These exclude presentation and are not frame rates.

## Render checks

Forward+ on the existing RTX 3070 Ti, private Xvfb; no user desktop focus/input.
`./tools/preview_isolated.sh -- --fungi-check` saves:

- `artifacts/fungi-01-ground.png`: ground-level caps/stems and clumps.
- `artifacts/fungi-02-overview.png`: emergent ring and paired B/R diagnostic maps.
- `artifacts/fungi-runtime.json` plus `.fungi.bin`: observed scene and model state.

The first inspection exposed a footer overlap and a clipped map legend; compact
HUD spacing and map offsets corrected them before final screenshots. The dedicated
assay is paused deliberately for reproducible inspection. Ordinary preview separately
checks live ecological integration; no paused image is evidence of evolving biology.

The completed ordinary preview advanced **45 ecological ticks / 1,182,600
biological seconds (~13.69 days)** and 633 water seconds. All three fungal plots
advanced with it; body count changed from 2,237 initial representatives (254
clumps) to 1,249 (148 clumps). Carbon residual 4.86e-10 kg; boundary warning false;
mean three-plot native-adapter step ~1.92 ms. Observed scene FPS was 21 in the
ordinary window and 17 under accelerated rain/dam; this is the whole current
forest, not a claimed marginal fungal-render cost or a performance target.
Both render logs and the complete check script finished without ERROR/SCRIPT ERROR.
