## Limits and continuation

Two-bucket soil, capped-conductivity infiltration, external deep drainage;
no Green–Ampt/Richards, lateral groundwater/baseflow, erosion, root uptake,
field calibration or offline Landlab comparison. Surface method is a modified
local-inertial scheme, not a full SWE solver or an exact Landlab port.
Presentation still has grid-dependent shorelines and decorative ripples.
Only synthetic grid/time sensitivity tested; 4 m is provisional for the valley.
GPU computation is not implemented. Hydrology save is not a whole-world save UI.

Next hydrology work: offline reference comparison, infiltration/baseflow,
watershed grid sensitivity, diagnostics/inspector and flow-aware presentation.
Tree ecology then animals; large style work waits for live trees, with Pavel's
reference link kept in DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md.
